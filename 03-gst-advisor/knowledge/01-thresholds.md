# GST registration thresholds (India)

## What "aggregate turnover" means

GST registration is triggered by **aggregate turnover**, counted **PAN-India**, across all supplies of a person with the same PAN — not one shop, not one app. Paytm GST Pulse only sees Paytm order/settlement data unless the merchant types other income (cash, other UPI apps, offline).

## Goods vs services

- **Goods**, most states: compulsory registration around **₹40 lakh** in a financial year (1 April–31 March).
- **Services**, most states: around **₹20 lakh**.
- These are the numbers GST Pulse uses in its rules engine. Confirm on the GST portal / a CA if your facts are unusual (e.g. already making interstate taxable supply, which can require registration regardless of turnover).

## Special-category states

Lower limits typically apply in Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim, Tripura, Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh:

- Goods: about **₹20 lakh**
- Services: about **₹10 lakh**

## What this product does not do

GST Pulse does not file returns and does not calculate the tax you will owe. It shows **where you stand vs the registration line** and explains next steps in plain language.
