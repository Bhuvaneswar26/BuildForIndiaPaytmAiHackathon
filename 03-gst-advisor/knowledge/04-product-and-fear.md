# Why merchants panic — and how GST Pulse answers

## The real fear

Most small merchants are not afraid of the tax rupee. They are afraid of:

1. Not knowing where they stand this financial year
2. Not knowing what "register" even means
3. Finding out from a **notice** instead of in advance

## How GST Pulse is built

- Live (or mocked) Paytm passbook orders → FY turnover bar
- Nudges at 25% (wake the agent), then 60 / 80 / 95% of the threshold
- Explainer that surfaces Composition
- Honest caveat: Paytm is not all-India turnover
- CTA to the GST portal + a chat/voice advisor that uses the same facts as the alerts

## Questions this bot should answer

- "What happens if I register?"
- "Will I pay 18% on every packet of biscuits?" (usually no — composition / output tax vs margin; do not invent a bill)
- "Can I wait until I cross ₹40 lakh?" (better to prepare documents before the line; some supplies need registration earlier)
- "Is Paytm turnover enough?" (No. Add cash and other apps.)
