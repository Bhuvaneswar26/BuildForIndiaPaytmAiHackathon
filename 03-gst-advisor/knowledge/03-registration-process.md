## Eligibility & Mandatory Registration

### Who Must Register for GST?

**Mandatory Registration Triggers**:

1. **Goods Suppliers**
   - Aggregate turnover exceeds **Rs 40 lakhs** per annum
   - Mandatory registration required once threshold crossed
   - Timeline: Register within **30 days** of liability arising

2. **Service Providers**
   - Aggregate turnover exceeds **Rs 20 lakhs** per annum
   - All service providers with turnover >Rs 20L must register
   - Timeline: Register within **30 days** of liability arising

3. **Special Category States** (Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, J&K, Ladakh, etc.)
   - Goods suppliers: Threshold is **Rs 10 lakhs**
   - Service providers: Threshold is **Rs 10 lakhs**
   - Lower thresholds in these states to encourage registration

4. **Inter-State Business**
   - Supply of goods from one state to another
   - Registration mandatory **regardless of turnover** (even Rs 1 turnover triggers requirement)
   - Service providers must register if annual turnover exceeds Rs 20 lakhs

5. **E-Commerce Platforms**
   - Any individual supplying goods/services through e-commerce platforms (Flipkart, Amazon, etc.)
   - Registration **mandatory regardless of turnover**
   - Sellers on marketplaces must register before starting sales

6. **Casual Taxable Persons**
   - Supply seasonally or intermittently through temporary stalls/shops
   - Registration mandatory **regardless of turnover**
   - Must register within **30 days** of commencing supply
   - 3-month validity period; can be renewed

7. **Non-Resident Taxable Persons**
   - Individuals located outside India supplying goods/services
   - Registration for online service providers
   - Must register if supplying to Indian customers

### Voluntary Registration

**Entities Below Threshold Can Voluntarily Register**:

- Turnover below Rs 40L (goods) or Rs 20L (services)
- Can opt for GST registration voluntarily
- Benefits: Input tax credit, B2B credibility, compliance readiness
- Once registered, must follow all GST compliance requirements
- Registration remains active for minimum 1 year

## Types of GST Registration

### 1. Normal Taxpayer

**Profile**:
- Standard GST registration for most businesses
- Applicable to taxpayers operating regular business in India
- Applies to both goods and service suppliers

**Eligibility**:
- Any entity not qualifying for Composition/Casual/Non-Resident categories
- Can be sole proprietorship, partnership, company, LLP, trust, etc.

**Key Features**:
- ✓ No deposit required (unlimited validity)
- ✓ Input tax credit allowed (claim GST paid on purchases)
- ✓ Must file monthly GSTR-1, GSTR-3B returns
- ✓ Full compliance requirements
- ✓ Can supply B2B and B2C

**Registration Validity**: Unlimited (active until voluntarily cancelled or revoked)

**When to Choose**: 
- Regular business operations
- Need input tax credit
- B2B transactions with GST-registered customers
- Manufacturing/wholesale businesses

---

### 2. Composition Taxpayer

**Profile**:
- Simplified GST scheme with flat tax rate
- Designed for small businesses with lower compliance burden

**Eligibility Criteria**:
- Turnover limit: **Rs 1.5 crores** per annum (aggregate turnover in last 4 quarters)
- Cannot opt for Composition if:
  - Supply involves inter-state transactions
  - Engaged in supply of services
  - Supply of goods exempted under GST
  - Taxable value >Rs 5 lakhs in a single inter-state transaction
  - Supplies through e-commerce platforms (generally not allowed)
  - Notified supplies like making of registered goods

**Key Features**:
- ✓ Flat GST rate (typically 1% for goods, 6% for services with turnover >50L)
- ✗ **NO input tax credit** (cannot claim GST paid on purchases)
- ✗ No B2B invoicing (no regular invoices to other registered businesses)
- ✓ Quarterly return filing (GSTR-4 once per quarter)
- ✓ Simpler compliance, lower documentation
- ✓ Lower compliance cost
- ✓ No audit requirements typically

**Timeline for Composition**:
- Opt during GST registration process
- Can be revoked at end of financial year only
- Cannot switch back to Normal Taxpayer during same financial year

**When to Choose**:
- Small retail/wholesale businesses
- Turnover < Rs 1.5 crore
- Can manage without input credit
- Want reduced compliance burden

**Financial Impact Example**:
```
Normal Taxpayer (5% GST rate):
Sales: Rs 100 lakhs
GST collected: Rs 5 lakhs
GST paid on purchases: Rs 2 lakhs
Net tax liability: Rs 3 lakhs

Composition Taxpayer (1% GST rate):
Sales: Rs 100 lakhs
GST to be paid: Rs 1 lakh (flat rate)
NO input credit claim possible
Savings: Rs 2 lakhs, but lose credit opportunity
```

### 3. Casual Taxable Person

**Profile**:
- Short-term, seasonal, or intermittent supply of goods/services
- Temporary stalls, festival shops, project-based work

**Eligibility**:
- Undertaking supply seasonally or intermittently
- Through temporary stall or shop
- Not regular/permanent business operation
- Mandatory registration regardless of turnover

**Key Features**:
- ✓ Deposit required: Amount equal to estimated GST liability
- ✓ Input tax credit allowed on deposits
- ✓ Registration validity: **3 months** (can be renewed)
- ✓ Monthly filing of GSTR-1, GSTR-3B returns
- ✓ Simplified documentation

**Deposit Mechanism**:
- Calculate estimated GST liability for registration period
- Deposit equivalent amount with GST authority
- Deposit returned after registration period ends
- If liability exceeds deposit, pay difference
- If liability less than deposit, refund issued

**Common Scenarios**:
- Festival/seasonal businesses
- Temporary event vendors
- Project-based contractors (short-term)
- Shop during fair/exhibition
- Wedding event catering
- Monsoon-only businesses

**When to Choose**:
- Operations <3 months per year
- Seasonal supply patterns
- Temporary business locations
- Project-based work

---

### 4. Non-Resident Taxable Person

**Profile**:
- Individual or entity located outside India
- Supply taxable goods/services to Indian recipients

**Eligibility**:
- Located outside India
- Supplying goods/services to India
- Can be individuals, companies, partnerships, etc.

**Key Features**:
- ✓ Special registration process for online service providers
- ✓ Input tax credit allowed
- ✓ Must file returns as per Indian GST rules
- ✓ Representation through authorized agent in India (optional but recommended)
- ✗ No physical business location required

**Sub-Categories**:
1. **Non-Resident Online Service Provider (NROSP)**
   - Supplies services digitally to India
   - Examples: Software consultancy, online training, digital products
   - Simplified registration process
   - Can file returns on behalf through agent

2. **UN Bodies/Embassies/Notified Persons**
   - UN organizations, embassies, international organizations
   - Special registration provisions
   - May have exemptions or special rates

---

### 5. SEZ (Special Economic Zone) Developers & Units

**Profile**:
- Businesses operating within Special Economic Zones

**Types**:
1. **SEZ Developer**
   - Develops infrastructure within SEZ
   - Supplies goods/services within SEZ
   - Special GST treatment for inter-SEZ supplies

2. **SEZ Unit**
   - Operates manufacturing/services within SEZ
   - Exempted from GST on supplies outside India
   - Concessional rates on domestic supplies
   - Requires SEZ certificate for registration

**Key Features**:
- ✓ Special registration process
- ✓ SEZ developer certificate required
- ✓ Location documents: SEZ approval certificate, allotment letter
- ✓ Different tax treatments compared to normal taxpayers

## 14-Step Online Registration Process

### Step 1: Access GST Portal

**Location**: services.gst.gov.in

**Process**:
1. Go to GST Portal homepage
2. Click on "Services" menu
3. Select "Registration"
4. Click on "New Registration"
5. System displays new GST registration form
6. Read acknowledgment message about registration process

**What Happens Next**: Portal generates Temporary Reference Number (TRN) instructions for next step

---

### Step 2: Generate TRN by Completing OTP Validation

**Purpose**: Secure email & mobile verification before form generation

**Form Fields to Complete**:

```
I am a*: [Taxpayer/Authorized Signatory/Authorized Representative]
         (Usually select "Taxpayer" for primary registration)

State/UT*: [Dropdown - Select business state]

District*: [Dropdown - Select district]

Legal Name of Business*: [Exactly as in PAN database]
                        [Portal auto-verifies against PAN]
                        [If mismatch, clarification needed]

Permanent Account Number (PAN)*: [10-digit PAN]
                                 [Business PAN or Proprietor PAN]
                                 [Link: "Click if PAN not available"]
                                 [Format: AAAAA1234B]

Email Address*: [Primary email for official GST communication]
                [OTP will be sent here]
                [IMPORTANT: Use email you can access]

Mobile Number*: [10-digit mobile number]
                [Separate OTP sent to this number]
                [Use verified mobile number]

CAPTCHA*: [Type characters shown in image]
          [Case-sensitive]
          [Refresh button to change]
```

**OTP Validation Process**:
1. Enter valid email address
2. Enter valid mobile number
3. Complete CAPTCHA verification
4. Click "PROCEED" button
5. Two OTPs generated (one for email, one for SMS)
6. **OTP validity**: 10 minutes only
7. Enter mobile OTP in first field
8. Enter email OTP in second field
9. Both OTPs must be validated to proceed

**Why This Matters**: 
- Prevents fraudulent registrations
- Ensures contact information accuracy
- Allows GST authority to reach applicant
- Establishes secure communication channel

**Common Issues**:
- ❌ OTP not received: Check spam folder; resend available
- ❌ OTP expired (>10 mins): Request new OTP
- ❌ Email/mobile mismatch: Ensure formats are correct
- ❌ PAN not verified: Allow 24 hours for PAN database sync

---

### Step 3: OTP Verification & TRN Generation

**Auto-Triggered After OTP Validation**:
1. System validates both OTPs
2. TRN (Temporary Reference Number) automatically generated
3. **TRN validity**: Until incomplete application expires (typically 30-40 days)
4. Confirmation email sent with TRN
5. Application saved as "Draft" status
6. Can be resumed using TRN anytime within validity period

**TRN Format**: Alphanumeric, used to access incomplete application

**Important**: Save TRN in secure location; needed to continue application if interrupted

---

### Step 4: TRN Login & Application Continuation

**Accessing Saved Application**:

1. Return to GST portal homepage
2. Go to "Services" → "Registration"
3. Select appropriate taxpayer category
4. Click on TRN field
5. Enter generated TRN
6. Complete CAPTCHA
7. Click "PROCEED"
8. Saved application loaded (at completion percentage shown)

**Dashboard Status**:
- Shows "My Saved Application"
- Displays creation date, form number (GST REG-01)
- Shows application expiry date
- Indicates completion percentage (e.g., 24% completed)
- Status shows as "Draft"

**Multi-Tab Navigation**:
```
Application Form Tabs (in sequence):

### Step 7: Submit Authorized Signatory Information (Tab 3)

**Purpose**: Identify person(s) authorized to sign GST documents, file returns, represent before GST authority

**Key Responsibility**: Authorized signatory is liable for:
- Filing GST returns
- Signing GST forms
- Communicating with GST authority
- GST compliance on behalf of business

**When Same Person for Multiple Roles**:
- If proprietor is also signatory: Repeat from Step 6
- If partner/director also signs: Mark checkbox "Also Authorized Signatory" in Step 6
- If different person: Add new person in this section

**For Companies/LLPs/Trusts**:
- Usually one of the directors/partners becomes signatory
- Can designate up to 2-3 signatories if needed

**Information Required** (same as promoters):
- Personal details (name, DOB, contact)
- Identity proof (Aadhaar, PAN, etc.)
- Photo (100 KB, JPG)
- Residential address
- Designation: "Authorized Signatory"

**Authorization Proof**:
1. **For Proprietor/Partnership**: Letter of Authorization on letterhead
2. **For Company/LLP**: Board Resolution or Partners Resolution
3. **For Trust**: Trustee Resolution

**Document Upload**:
- File type: PDF, JPG
- Size: 100 KB max
- Content: Letter/Resolution authorizing signatory role

### Step 8: Submit Authorized Representative Details (Tab 4)

**Purpose**: Optional; if hiring external professional (CA, consultant) to manage GST

**When Needed**:
- Company wants external CA to file returns
- Partnership engaging professional for GST compliance
- Large entity with specialized GST team
- Non-resident person filing through representative

**Who Can Be Authorized Representative**:
- Chartered Accountant (CA)
- Company Secretary (CS)
- Cost Accountant (CA)
- Tax professional with ARN (Active Registration Number)
- Relative or employee (though professional preferred)

**Information to Provide**:
```
Representative Details:
├─ Full Name
├─ ARN (Active Registration Number) - if applicable
├─ PAN/Aadhaar
├─ Mobile & Email
├─ Residential Address
├─ Designation/Profession
└─ Authorization proof (if representative filing returns)
```

**When NOT Needed**:
- Proprietor handling own compliance
- Partner/Director managing registration
- Business doesn't need external help

**Authorization Letter**:
If appointing representative:
- Letter on business letterhead
- Signed by authorized signatory
- States representative name and role
- Scope of authority (filing returns, responding to queries, etc.)
- Validity period

## Advanced Rules & Fast-Track Options

### Rule 9A: Automatic Registration Within 3 Working Days

**Eligibility**:
- All taxpayers can benefit
- Not restricted to Composition/Casual categories
- Automatic approval based on system-generated risk analysis

**Process**:
1. **Application submitted** via standard process
2. **System conducts** automatic validation:
   - PAN/Aadhaar verification
   - Address match against databases
   - Document quality checks
   - Data consistency verification
3. **Risk scoring**: Assigned by automated algorithm
4. **Low-risk applications**: Auto-approved within 3 working days
5. **High-risk applications**: Flagged for manual review (RFI issued)

**Benefits**:
- ✓ Faster registration possible
- ✓ GSTIN issued without wait
- ✓ Immediate compliance capability
- ✓ Applies even without opting for Rule 14A

**Conditions**:
- Must meet all data validation requirements
- Address must be verified in government databases
- PAN/Aadhaar must match exactly
- No document discrepancies

**Non-Qualifying Applications for Rule 9A**:
- Missing or unclear documents
- Address not verifiable in government records
- PAN/Aadhaar name mismatch
- Inconsistent information across fields
- New/recent business with limited records

---

### Rule 14A: Simplified GST Registration with Aadhaar Authentication

**Latest Update** (from document): **Auto-approval system begins Nov 1, 2025**

**Eligibility**:
- Estimated monthly output tax liability **not exceeding Rs 2.5 lakhs**
- B2B (GST-registered customer) supplies only
- Aadhaar authentication completed for signatory + at least one promoter

**Benefits**:
- ✓ Fast-track processing
- ✓ Aadhaar-based authentication (Aadhaar eSign)
- ✓ Reduced document scrutiny
- ✓ Faster GSTIN issuance
- ✓ Recommended for low-liability businesses

**How It Works**:

```
RULE 14A OPT-IN PROCESS:

Step 1: On Application Form
├─ Locate "Rule 14A Opt-In" checkbox
├─ Select YES to opt for simplified registration
└─ Note: One registration per State/UT per PAN only

Step 2: Aadhaar Authentication Requirement
├─ Primary Authorized Signatory: Mandatory
├─ At least One Promoter/Partner: Mandatory
└─ Must complete Aadhaar eSign/OTP verification

Step 3: After Application Submission
├─ System verifies Aadhaar details
├─ Matches with signatory/promoter records
├─ No manual verification for low-risk cases
└─ Auto-approval triggered if all conditions met

Step 4: Approval Timeline
├─ Low-risk: Approved within 3 days
├─ Moderate risk: 7-15 days (may need clarification)
├─ High-risk: Referred for manual review
└─ GSTIN issued upon approval
```

**Limitation**:
- ⚠️ Only one registration per State per PAN
- ⚠️ Cannot file multiple registrations in same state under Rule 14A
- ⚠️ B2B liability only (output tax to GST-registered buyers)
- ⚠️ Once opted, Aadhaar authentication mandatory for signatory/promoter

**Future Enhancement** (mentioned in document):
- From **November 1, 2025**: New auto-approval system begins
- Processes simplified further
- Majority of low-risk applications approved within 3 days
- Benefits all applicants (not just Rule 14A)

## Common FAQs

### 1. Aggregate Turnover Limit for Mandatory GST Registration

**Answer**:
- **Goods suppliers**: Rs 40 lakhs per annum
- **Service providers**: Rs 20 lakhs per annum
- **Special category states**: Rs 10 lakhs (both goods & services)
- **E-commerce sellers**: Mandatory regardless of turnover
- **Inter-state suppliers**: Mandatory regardless of turnover

**Turnover Calculation**:
- Includes all taxable supplies + exempt supplies
- Includes supplies in India + outside India (in some cases)
- Excludes: GST amount itself, returns/refunds
- Calculated on financial year basis (April - March in India)

---

### 2. Casual Taxable Person Validity Period

**Answer**:
- Registration validity: **3 months** from date of approval
- Not renewable during same financial year
- Can be re-registered in next financial year if seasonal business continues
- Deposit mechanism: Equal to estimated GST liability for 3-month period

**Example Scenario**:
```
Temporary Shop Started: July 1, 2024
CTP Registration Approved: July 10, 2024
Validity Period: July 10 - October 10, 2024 (3 months)
Deposit Refunded: After Oct 10, 2024

If business continues next season:
├─ New CTP registration in July 2025
├─ Fresh deposit required
└─ 3-month validity from new approval date
```

---

### 3. Voluntary Registration Below Threshold

**Answer**:
- **Yes**, any entity can voluntarily register even if below threshold
- No mandatory registration requirement if below limits
- Benefits of voluntary registration:
  - Input tax credit eligibility
  - Credibility with B2B customers
  - Compliance readiness
  - Can supply to GST-registered businesses

**Drawback**:
- Once registered, must follow all GST compliance
- Cannot de-register within 1 year
- Monthly return filing mandatory
- Higher compliance cost

---

### 4. Required Documents for GST Registration

**Quick Answer**:
- Proof of business constitution (1 document)

- Authorization: Letter/Board Resolution (1 document)

- Bank account proof: Passbook/Cancelled cheque (1 document)

**Best Practice**: Provide 3-4 documents per location to reduce rejections

### 6. Separate GST Registration for Each State

**Answer**:
- **Yes**, business needs separate registration for each state where it operates
- Each state = separate GSTIN
- Each state GSTIN links to same PAN
- All GST INs must be integrated if centralizing

**When Multiple Registrations Needed**:
- Manufacturing unit in State A + warehouse in State B
- Multiple retail stores across different states
- Head office in one state + branch in another
- Service centers in multiple states

**Registration Process**:
- File separate applications for each state
- Same documents required for each state
- Each gets unique GSTIN (state-code + unique number)

**Limitation Under Rule 14A**:
- Only ONE registration per State per PAN (if opting for Rule 14A)
- Cannot file multiple simplified registrations in same state
- Must use standard process for additional registrations in same state

### 7. GST Registration Failure - Late Filing Penalties

**Answer**:
If business fails to obtain GST registration within 30 days of liability arising:

**Penalties**:
- Late fee: Rs 100 per day (up to Rs 10,000 maximum for regular taxpayers)
- Casual/Non-resident: Up to Rs 5,000
- Recovery: Can be recovered through demand notice
- Payment: Before registration approved

**Consequences**:
- Turnover after 30-day period: Fully taxable without input credit
- Cannot claim retrospective credit on purchases before registration
- GST authority may levy interest on unpaid tax

**Compliance Action**:
- Immediate registration recommended upon crossing threshold
- Maintain records of when turnover was exceeded
- Keep proof of registration application date
- Calculate GST liability from liability date, not registration date

---

### 8. Aadhaar Requirement for GST Registration

**Answer**:
- **Optional but recommended** during standard registration
- **Mandatory** if opting for Rule 14A (simplified registration)

**Benefits of Providing Aadhaar**:
- ✓ Faster verification process

- ✓ Aadhaar eSign available for digital signing

**If Aadhaar Not Provided**:
- ✓ Can still register using alternative identity proofs
- ✓ Manual verification may be needed
- ✓ Processing time may extend
- ✓ Rule 14A benefits not available

**Answer**:
- If operating multiple businesses: Usually requires separate GST registration
- Each business = separate PAN preferably
- Each business entity = separate GSTIN

**Exception**:
- Same business operating from multiple locations: Single GSTIN with additional places
- Related entities under same umbrella: Can opt for single or multiple GST INs

**Examples**:

├─ GSTR-3B: 20th of next month
└─ Auto-generation: GSTR-2A on 12th

Quarterly Filing (Composition taxpayers):
├─ GSTR-4: 18th after quarter-end

Quarterly Audit (Turnover >50L):
├─ Deadline: 30 days before return due date
└─ Audit report required

Annual Filing (All taxpayers):
├─ GSTR-9: 31st December of next financial year
├─ GSTR-9C (if Audit required): Same deadline
└─ Original books to be maintained

Amendment/Rectification (Any year):
├─ GSTR-1 amended: Within one year
├─ GSTR-3B amended: As per rules
└─ Retrospective corrections: GSTR-5/7/10 forms
```

## Document Quality Standards

### For All Uploaded Documents

**Format Requirements**:
- **Acceptable**: PDF, JPG, JPEG
- **Not Acceptable**: PNG, TIFF, Word, Excel, or other formats
- **Size Limits**: 100 KB (photos), 1 MB (agreements/documents)

**Quality Standards**:
- **Resolution**: Minimum 150 DPI (High-res scans recommended)
- **Clarity**: Text must be clearly readable
- **Alignment**: Documents should be straight (not skewed)
- **Legibility**: All important details visible and legible
- **Completeness**: Entire document visible (not cropped at edges)

**Compression Methods**:
- Online tools: tinypdf.com, compressor.io, ilovepdf.com
- Local software: Preview (Mac), Photos App (Windows 10+)
- Command line: ImageMagick, GhostScript
