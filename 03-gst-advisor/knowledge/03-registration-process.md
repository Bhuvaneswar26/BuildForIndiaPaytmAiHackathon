# What GST registration actually involves

## It is a portal process, not a raid

Registration is done on the government GST portal. GST Pulse only deep-links there and lists documents. We do not submit the form for you.

## Documents merchants should keep ready

- PAN of the business or proprietor
- Bank account proof (cancelled cheque or passbook first page)
- Proof of principal place of business (electricity bill, property tax, or registered rent agreement)
- Photograph
- Aadhaar of the authorised signatory (OTP on the portal)

## After registration, filing in plain words

- **Regular scheme**: typically monthly returns (and an annual return). You collect GST, claim input credit where eligible, and pay the difference.
- **Composition**: typically a simpler quarterly statement/payment. You usually cannot issue tax invoices that pass input credit to buyers.

## Cost

Government registration fee is not the scary part. Time, bookkeeping, and (if you want) a CA are the real costs. Composition exists to shrink that burden for small sellers.
