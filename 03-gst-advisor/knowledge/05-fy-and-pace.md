# Financial year, monthly vs annual, and pace

## Financial year

Indian GST FY runs **1 April to 31 March**. GST Pulse sums SUCCESS Paytm orders in that window.

## Monthly vs annual

The registration test is **annual aggregate**, but monthly sums help a merchant see seasonality (Diwali, school season). Crossing 60% in August with a steep slope is a different story than a flat year.

## Pace ("you may cross in ~N months")

GST Pulse draws a straight line from FY start to today. It is a **projection**, not a prediction and not a notice. If the merchant's other-income field is empty, the projection is even more incomplete.

## Checkpoints

- 25%: start classifying risk, optional soft WhatsApp
- 60%: explain registration + composition
- 80%: plan portal this month
- 95%: start-now reminder, still not a government notice
