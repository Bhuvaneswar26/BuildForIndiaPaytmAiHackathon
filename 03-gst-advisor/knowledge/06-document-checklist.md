# GST Registration Document Identification & Preparation Guide

## Overview

This skill helps businesses, compliance officers, and advisors identify all required documents for GST (Goods and Services Tax) registration in India. It categorizes documents by business structure, specifies technical requirements (file types, sizes), and clarifies submission constraints.

## Section 1: Proof of Constitution of Business

### Purpose
Establish the legal foundation and structure of the business entity applying for GST registration.

### Applicable Documents by Business Type

#### For Partnerships
- **Partnership Deed**
  - File type: JPG, PDF
  - Max size: 1 MB per document
  - Multiple uploads: NO (single document required)
  - Why: Establishes partnership structure, partner identities, and profit/loss sharing ratios

#### For Trusts
- **Trust Deed**
  - File type: JPG, PDF
  - Max size: 1 MB per document
  - Multiple uploads: NO (single document required)
  - Why: Proves trust establishment and beneficiary/trustee designations

#### For Private/Public Companies
- **Certificate of Incorporation**
  - File type: JPG, PDF
  - Max size: 1 MB per document
  - Multiple uploads: NO (single document required)
  - Why: Official proof from Registrar of Companies (ROC) showing company formation

#### For Proprietorships & Alternative Structures
- **Any Proof Substantiating Constitution**
  - File type: JPG, PDF
  - Max size: 1 MB per document
  - Multiple uploads: NO (single document required)
  - Acceptable alternatives:
    - Business registration certificate
    - Trade license
    - UdyamReg (MSME registration)
    - Any government-issued business registration document

#### For Established Entities with Recent Changes
- **Certificate for Establishment**
  - File type: JPG, PDF
  - Max size: 1 MB per document
  - Multiple uploads: NO (single document required)
  - Why: For businesses that have been operational for years; proves current legal standing

#### For Entities with Prior Registrations
- **Registration Certificate**
  - File type: JPG, PDF
  - Max size: 1 MB per document
  - Multiple uploads: NO (single document required)
  - Acceptable: Sales tax certificates, excise registration, prior GST registration (if applying for new GSTIN)

### Key Requirements for This Section

✓ **Document must be original or certified copy** — photocopies without certification may be rejected
✓ **Must be current** — issued recently enough to reflect current business structure
✓ **Language**: English or Hindi (certified translation for other languages)
✓ **Clarity**: Text must be readable; scanned documents must be high-resolution (≥300 DPI)

### Common Scenarios & Documentation

| Business Type | Primary Document | Alternatives |
|---|---|---|
| Sole Proprietorship | UdyamReg or Trade License | Aadhar + PAN + Business proof |
| Partnership | Partnership Deed (registered) | Partnership agreement + identity proofs of all partners |
| LLP | Certificate of Incorporation from MCA | LLP Agreement |
| Private Company | Certificate of Incorporation (ROC) | Memorandum & Articles of Association |
| Public Company | Certificate of Incorporation (ROC) | Board Resolution nominating applicant |
| Trust | Trust Deed (registered) | Deed of Settlement + Board Resolution |
| Cooperative Society | Registration Certificate | Bye-laws + Board Resolution |

## Section 2: Stakeholder & Signatory Identification

### 2.1 Photo of Stakeholder

**Purpose**: Identity verification for key stakeholder/director/partner/trustee

**Technical Specifications**:
- File type: JPG
- Max size: 100 KB per document
- Multiple uploads: NO
- Acceptable formats: Colored or B&W photograph

**Requirements**:
- Head-shot style photo (face clearly visible)
- Recent photo (within 6 months recommended)
- Professional quality (not a screenshot or webcam capture)
- Proper lighting to ensure facial features are distinct
- Dimensions: Typically passport-size (3.5" × 4.25" or 2" × 2")

**Common rejection reasons**:
- File exceeds 100 KB (compress using online tools if needed)
- Face not clearly visible or partially obscured
- Photo too dark or overexposed
- Multiple people in the frame
- Wearing sunglasses or hat that obscures face

### 2.2 Photo of Authorised Signatory

**Purpose**: Verification of the person authorized to sign GST applications and documents

**Technical Specifications**:
- File type: JPG
- Max size: 100 KB per document
- Multiple uploads: NO
- Same photo requirements as stakeholder

**Key difference**: 
- Must be the person who will execute GST-related documents
- If signatory is different from stakeholder, both photos required
- In corporate structures, typically the authorized Director/Partner

## Section 3: Proof of Appointment of Authorised Signatory

### Purpose
Establish the authority of the signatory to bind the business in GST matters and sign documents.

### 3.1 Letter of Authorisation

**Technical Specifications**:
- File type: JPG, PDF
- Max size: 100 KB per document
- Multiple uploads: YES (multiple signatories can be authorized)

**Content Requirements**:
- On business letterhead
- Clearly identifies authorized signatory (name, designation, ID)
- Specifies authority to sign GST-related documents
- Signed by proprietor/partner/director/trustee
- Date of authorization
- Validity period (recommended: multi-year)

**Template outline**:
```
[Business Name & Address]
[Date]

TO WHOM IT MAY CONCERN

This is to certify that Mr./Ms. [Name], [Designation], is hereby 
authorized to represent [Business Name] and sign all documents 
related to Goods and Services Tax (GST) registration and compliance.

This authorization is valid from [Date] to [Date].

Signed
[Authorized Signatory with official seal]
```

### 3.2 Copy of Resolution (for Companies/LLPs/Trusts)

**Technical Specifications**:
- File type: JPG, PDF
- Max size: 100 KB per document
- Multiple uploads: YES

**Applicable to**:
- Private/Public Companies → Board of Directors Resolution
- LLPs → Partners' Resolution
- Trusts → Trustee Board Resolution
- Cooperative Societies → Management Committee Resolution

**Resolution must include**:
- Date of Board/Partners meeting
- Resolution number and details
- Clear identification of authorized signatory(ies)
- Authority scope and limitations
- Board/Committee seal and signatures
- Acceptance letter (optional but recommended) — signatory confirming acceptance of authority

**Standard format**:
```
BOARD RESOLUTION

At the Board Meeting held on [Date]:

RESOLVED THAT:
Mr./Ms. [Name], [Designation], be and is hereby authorized to 
represent the company and sign all GST-related documents and 
returns for [GSTIN/Business Name].

FURTHER RESOLVED THAT:
The company shall indemnify the above person against all acts 
done in good faith for GST compliance.

Board Seal: ________________
Director 1: ________________  Date: ________
Director 2: ________________  Date: ________
```

## Section 4: Proof of Principal Place of Business

### Purpose
Establish the location where business is primarily conducted; GST authority will correspond and conduct inspections at this address.

### Critical Importance
- **Only ONE principal place of business** can be declared per GSTIN
- Must be actual operational location (not just mailing address)
- Physical verification may be conducted by GST authorities
- All business activities primarily conducted here should be mentioned

### Acceptable Proof Documents

**Multiple uploads: YES** (multiple documents for the same location strengthen the application)

#### 1. Property Tax Receipt

**Technical Specifications**:
- File type: JPG, PDF
- Max size: 100 KB per document
- Frequency: Annual or latest assessment

**Acceptable documents**:
- Municipal Corporation property tax receipt
- Municipal Council property tax receipt
- Gram Panchayat assessment certificate
- Recent property tax assessment (current financial year preferred)

**What it establishes**: Ownership or long-term occupancy of the premises

#### 2. Municipal Khata Copy

**Technical Specifications**:
- File type: JPG, PDF
- Max size: 100 KB per document

**What it is**: Municipal/City Corporation property registry record

**Acceptable documents**:
- Khata extract/certificate (Karnataka, Telangana)
- Ward record copy (Andhra Pradesh)
- Municipal registry confirmation
- Property identification record from urban local body

**Regional variations** (same document, different names):
- Karnataka: "Khata Certificate"
- Telangana: "Khata Patta"
- Tamil Nadu: "Chitta/Adangal"
- Maharashtra: "Mhal Patra"
- Delhi: "Property Identification Number (PIN) certificate"

#### 3. Electricity Bill

**Technical Specifications**:
- File type: JPG, PDF
- Max size: 100 KB per document
- Frequency: Most recent bill (ideally last 2-3 months)

**Acceptable utility bills**:
- Electricity connection in business name or proprietor name
- Recent bill showing complete address and account number
- Bill status: Active (not suspended or disconnected)
- Post-paid or prepaid bills acceptable

**Regional electricity boards**:
- BESCOM (Karnataka), TSSPDCL (Telangana), TNEB (Tamil Nadu), MSEDCL (Maharashtra), etc.

**Why it works**: Proves regular occupancy and active business operations

#### 4. Rent/Lease Agreement

**Technical Specifications**:
- File type: JPG, PDF
- Max size: 1 MB per document
- Frequency: Original agreement (not copy)

**Key details to include in the agreement**:
- Lessor (property owner) name and address
- Lessee (business/entrepreneur) name
- Property location and full address
- Lease period (should have remaining term)
- Monthly/annual rent amount
- Commencement date
- Signature of both lessor and lessee
- Stamp paper (should be on proper stamp paper in India)

**Preferred format**:
- Agreement on stamp paper with stamp duty paid
- Notarized agreement
- Registered deed (highest acceptance)

**Common issues**:
- ❌ Agreement not on stamp paper (rejected)
- ❌ Agreement in old name if business has changed names (clarification required)
- ❌ Agreement for property not yet occupied (need physical evidence of possession)

#### 5. Consent Letter

**Technical Specifications**:
- File type: JPG, PDF
- Max size: 100 KB per document

**Applicable scenarios**:
- When property owner is different from business operator
- Required for rented premises
- Needed when using family property without formal lease

**Content requirements**:
- On letterhead or plain paper (signed and dated)
- Property owner's name, address, contact
- Business/Applicant's name
- Consent for using the property for business operations
- Specific business activity mentioned
- Validity period of consent
- Owner's signature and seal (if applicable)

**Template**:
```
CONSENT LETTER

Date: [Date]

TO WHOM IT MAY CONCERN

This is to certify that I, [Property Owner Name], am the 
owner/possessor of the property located at [Full Address].

I hereby consent to [Business Name]/[Proprietor Name] using 
the above-mentioned premises for [Nature of Business] 
operations and GST registration.

This consent is valid from [Date] to [Date].

Signature: ________________
[Property Owner Name]
[Contact Number]
[Aadhaar/Voter ID Number]
```

#### 6. Documents/Certificate Issued by Government

**Technical Specifications**:
- File type: JPG, PDF
- Max size: 100 KB per document

**Acceptable government certificates**:
- **NOC (No Objection Certificate)** from Municipal Authority for business premises
- **Occupancy Certificate** from Municipal Corporation or Development Authority
- **Building Completion Certificate** from urban development authority
- **Use Permission Certificate** from civic body
- **Industrial Estate Allotment Certificate** (for industrial units)
- **SEZ Certificate** (for Special Economic Zone units)
- **Export House Certificate** (for export-oriented businesses)

**Regional certifications**:
- Development Authority registration (NRRDA, DMRC, etc. in Delhi)
- Improvement Trust certificates (in older metros)
- Metropolitan Planning Authority approvals

## Section 5: Proof of Additional Place of Business

### Purpose
If the business operates from multiple locations, each location requires separate documentation.

### Key Guidelines

- **Locations with business activity** → Must be declared and documented
- **Each location gets unique documentation** → Same types of docs as principal place
- **Multiple documents per location strengthened case** → Use property tax + electricity + other proof
- **Locations can be added later** → Through GST return amendments (GSTR-1)

### Technical Specifications for Each Location

**Multiple uploads: YES** — provide multiple proofs per additional location

### Documents Required (Same as Principal Place)

Each additional business location must be supported by:

| Document Type | Format | Size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Property Tax Receipt | JPG, PDF | 100 KB | Current year preferred |
| Municipal Khata Copy | JPG, PDF | 100 KB | Official registry extract |
| Electricity Bill | JPG, PDF | 100 KB | Recent bill (2-3 months old) |
| Rent/Lease Agreement | JPG, PDF | 1 MB | On stamp paper, recent date |
| Consent Letter | JPG, PDF | 100 KB | If property owner ≠ business operator |
| Government Certificate | JPG, PDF | 100 KB | NOC, Occupancy Certificate, etc. |

### Declaration Requirements

For each additional place:
- Exact location/address
- Nature of business activity at that location
- Date of commencement at this location
- Whether storage/warehouse or operational location
- Any other business registered at this location

### Common Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Manufacturing unit + Warehouse**
- Manufacturing location (principal): Lease agreement + Electricity bill + Property tax
- Warehouse location (additional): Consent from owner + Electricity bill + Municipal record

**Scenario 2: Retail showroom + Service center**
- Main showroom (principal): All documents
- Service center in different city (additional): Lease + Consent letter + Electricity + Khata

**Scenario 3: Delivery hub + Collection points**
- Collection points: If manned by staff → Treat as business location; if automated/unmanned → May not require documentation
- Delivery hubs: Usually documented if employees present

## Section 6: Supporting Documents for Clarifications & Amendments

### Purpose
Submitted when GST officer raises queries (RFI - Request for Information) or when filing clarifications on existing applications.

### Technical Specifications

- File type: JPG, PDF
- Max size: 1 MB per document
- Multiple uploads: YES (bundle related documents together)

### Types of Supporting Documents

#### A. Business Registration & Compliance Documents
- Previous GST registrations (if returning taxpayer)
- Sales tax/VAT certificates (old regime)
- Excise registration certificates
- Service tax registration proof
- Business license/Trade license
- NOC from industry association (if applicable)

#### B. Bank & Financial Documents
- Bank statement (to show business activity)
- Business loan/credit facility approval letter
- Investment proof or capital infusion letter
- Balance sheet or accounting records (if requested)

#### C. Activity/Operations Proof
- Invoices issued (copies of recent invoices to establish business activity)
- Purchase orders or supply contracts
- Customer/supplier letters (establishing business operations)
- Website/social media proof (for online businesses)
- Import/Export license (if applicable)

#### D. Tax & Regulatory Compliance
- Income Tax Return filing confirmation
- Tax Audit Certificate (for large businesses)
- Form 10A (if eligible for exemption)
- Udyam Registration Certificate (MSME businesses)
- Professional license/certificate (if service provider)

#### E. Address Verification Supplements
- Affidavit on stamp paper (if original location documents unavailable)
- Notarized letter from landlord (if formal lease unavailable)
- Photographs of premises (showing business operations)
- Google Maps/Satellite imagery evidence (for location verification)

#### F. Signatory Authority Supplements
- Copy of signatory's PAN card
- Copy of signatory's Aadhaar card
- Director's identification from ROC (for companies)
- Partnership deed excerpt (for partnerships)
- Trust document excerpt (for trusts)

#### G. Clarification-Specific Documents
- Response letter to GST Officer queries (if any filed)
- Amended financial statements (if correcting turnover claims)
- Corrected utility bills (if initial bill rejected)
- Additional property documents (if initial proof insufficient)
- Supplementary consent letters (if location co-occupancy clarification needed)

### Filing Clarifications Best Practices

**When submitting supporting documents:**
1. **Organize by category** → Group all location docs, all compliance docs, etc.
2. **Use cover letter** → List all submitted documents with brief explanation
3. **Number documents** → For easy reference by GST Officer
4. **Respond to specific query** → Address each GST Officer query point-by-point
5. **Timeline** → Usually GST authorities provide 7-15 days to respond
6. **Channel** → Typically uploaded through GST portal or submitted via post to local jurisdiction

## File Format & Technical Requirements Summary

### Accepted File Formats

| Document Category | Format | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Most documents | JPG, PDF | Both formats equally acceptable |
| Photos | JPG | Preferred format for photos |
| Agreements | PDF | Preferred for multi-page documents |
| Certificates | JPG or PDF | Either acceptable |

### File Size Limits

| Document Type | Max Size | Compression Tips |
|---|---|---|
| Individual photos | 100 KB | Use online JPEG compressor (tinypng.com, compressor.io) |
| Scanned documents | 1 MB | Reduce DPI from 300 to 150; remove color if B&W |
| Large agreements | 1 MB | Split into multiple pages if necessary |
| Utility bills | 100 KB | Usually already small; crop to relevant portion |
| Property tax receipts | 100 KB | Crop page to remove headers/footers |

### Image Quality Standards

- **Resolution**: Minimum 150 DPI (300 DPI for critical documents)
- **Legibility**: All text must be readable (minimum font size ~8pt visible)
- **Angle**: Document straight (not skewed or at angle)
- **Color**: Original or B&W acceptable (avoid sepia tones)
- **Background**: Plain background preferred; avoid shadows/glare
- **Cropping**: Include entire document; minimize empty margins

### Common File Size Reduction Methods

```
For PDF files:
- Open in Adobe Reader → Print to PDF with compression
- Use online tools: ilovepdf.com, smallpdf.com
- Command line: gs -sDEVICE=pdfwrite -dQuality=80 old.pdf -o new.pdf

For JPG images:
- Online: tinypng.com, compressor.io, squoosh.app
- Local: ImageMagick: convert image.jpg -quality 80 compressed.jpg
- Windows: Built-in Photos app → Edit → Compress
```

## Common Document Issues & Solutions

| Issue | Cause | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| File exceeds size limit | High-resolution scan or multiple pages | Use online compression tools; split large documents |
| Text not readable | Low DPI scan (under 150 DPI) | Re-scan at 150-200 DPI; ensure good lighting |
| Document rejected - "Not recent" | Document issued >2 years ago | Obtain fresh certificate; some authorities accept older docs if still valid |
| Lease agreement not accepted | Not on stamp paper | Get agreement registered with local authority; obtain stamp paper copy |
| Address mismatch | Different addresses in different documents | File clarification with affidavit explaining change of premises |
| Utility bill in different name | Electricity connection in owner's name, not applicant's | File consent letter from property owner; some utilities accept letter |
| Multiple signatories needed | Business has multiple authorized persons | Submit resolution listing all signatories; authorization for each |
| Proof of business activity missing | New business with no operational proof | Submit bank statements, orders, supply contracts, website screenshots |

## GST Registration Document Submission Workflow

### Step 1: Self-Assessment (Pre-Application)
- Identify business structure
- Determine principal and additional places of business
- Identify authorized signatory
- Gather all required proof documents

### Step 2: Document Compilation
- Scan/photograph all documents in correct format
- Ensure file sizes within limits
- Verify image quality and readability
- Create organized folder structure (by category)

### Step 3: Portal Upload
- Login to GST portal (services.gst.gov.in)
- Navigate to "Registration" → "New Registration"
- Fill application form with business details
- Upload documents as per portal prompts
- System validates format and size (automatic)

### Step 4: Portal Checks
- Portal performs format validation (file type, size)
- Portal performs automatic address checks (against available databases)
- Confirmation page generated with reference number
- Email sent with submission details

### Step 5: GST Authority Review (3-30 days typically)
- **No issues**: Registration granted (GSTIN generated)
- **Minor issues**: RFI (Request for Information) issued → 7-15 days to respond
- **Major discrepancies**: Registration rejected (reasons provided)

### Step 6: Clarification (if RFI Issued)
- GST Officer identifies specific document/information needed
- Applicant submits supporting documents through portal
- Address verification may be conducted (physical inspection)
- Final decision within 7 days of receiving clarification

### Step 7: Post-Registration
- GSTIN issued (14-digit unique number)
- Certificate of Registration generated
- Access to GST return filing portal
- First return due by 20th of following month

## Document Storage & Record Management

### Retention Period
- **All GST documents**: Retain for 6 years from date of registration
- **Supporting documents**: Retain as long as relevant to registration
- **Original documents**: Keep in secure location (not necessarily submitted)

### Backup Strategy
- Maintain digital copies (encrypted USB, cloud storage)
- Keep photocopies of physical documents
- Store scanned versions with good metadata (date, doc type)
- Document checklist for audit trail

### Security Considerations
- Sensitive documents (deeds, agreements): Keep original in safe
- Scanned copies: Use password-protected PDFs
- Financial documents: Segregate from public-facing documents
- Photos: Remove if not needed; use low-resolution versions for storage

## Quick Reference: What to Include in Application Package

### Mandatory (Every Application)
1. Proof of Constitution (1 doc)
2. Signatory photo (1 JPG)
3. Stakeholder photo (1 JPG)
4. Authorization proof (1-2 docs)
5. Principal place proofs (2-3 docs minimum)

### Conditional (Based on Business Type)
- Board/Partners Resolution (if company/LLP/trust)
- Lease agreement (if renting)
- Consent letter (if using others' property)
- Industry-specific licenses (if applicable)

### Recommended (Strengthens Application)
- Multiple proofs for same location (3-4 docs for principal place)
- Bank statement showing business activity
- Previous tax registrations
- Website/social media evidence (for online businesses)
- Certified copies (instead of plain photocopies)

## Notes for Professionals & Advisors

### When Advising Clients

1. **Pre-filing review**: Audit all documents for completeness, clarity, and compliance
2. **Common risks**: Incomplete applications lead to RFI/rejection; plan for 10-15 day RFI resolution
3. **Timeline management**: Budget 45-60 days for complete registration (filing + approval + GSTIN issuance)
4. **Escalation path**: If RFI issued, advisor should coordinate response with client within 7 days
5. **Multiple locations**: Each location adds 2-3 documents; plan accordingly

### For Compliance & Audit
- Maintain "Registration File" with all submitted and supporting documents
- File GST annual reports (GSTR-9) with reference to registration documents
- Address verification may occur anytime during first 5 years of registration
- Keep all proofs updated if location/signatory changes occur
