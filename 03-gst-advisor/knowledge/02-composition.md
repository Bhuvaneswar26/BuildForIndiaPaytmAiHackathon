# Composition Scheme — the relief valve most kirana owners have not heard of

## Idea in one sentence

If you are a small supplier, you may opt for the **Composition Scheme**: a simplified, roughly flat rate and **quarterly** payment/statement instead of the heavier regular monthly GST compliance.

## Typical eligibility (confirm current notifications)

- **Goods / manufacturers / traders**: turnover usually up to **₹1.5 crore**.
- **Services** (and some restaurant supplies): a lower cap, often around **₹50 lakh**.
- You generally cannot collect GST on invoices the same way as a regular taxpayer; you pay a composition rate on turnover instead.
- Illustrative rates merchants hear: about **1%** for traders/manufacturers and about **6%** for services — **always verify the current rate** before opting.

## How to talk about it

Do not say "GST means high tax." Say: "Registering is a legal checkbox. Many small goods businesses then ask a CA whether composition is cheaper and simpler than regular GST."

## Who should not assume composition

Interstate suppliers, some e-commerce operators, and businesses that need to issue tax invoices for B2B input credit may need the regular scheme. GST Pulse should send those merchants to a CA / the portal, not decide for them.
