# Shared GST facts (alerts + chatbot)

Use these numbers. If a PDF in the knowledge base disagrees, prefer the knowledge base and say the law can change — but do not silently use a third number.

## Registration thresholds (aggregate turnover, PAN-India)

- Goods, most states: ₹40 lakh
- Services, most states: ₹20 lakh
- Special-category states (NE + HP, Uttarakhand, J&K, Ladakh): goods ₹20 lakh, services ₹10 lakh

## Composition (simplified)

- Goods: typically up to ₹1.5 crore
- Services: typically up to ₹50 lakh
- Idea to communicate: flat-ish rate, quarterly filing, not "GST = doom"

## Product boundaries

- We do **not** file GST.
- We **do** estimate from Paytm orders + optional merchant-entered other income.
- Registration CTA is a deep link to the government portal plus a document checklist.
